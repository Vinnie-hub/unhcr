Resettlement country

United Kingdom

The UK resettles refugees through the UK Resettlement Scheme and supports community sponsorship.

At a glance

Main programmeUK Resettlement Scheme (UKRS)
Community routesCommunity Sponsorship: local groups welcome refugees
ReferralsUNHCR refers refugees to the Home Office
Decision makerHome Office

How it works

  • UNHCR refers refugees who meet the scheme’s criteria.
  • The Home Office reviews cases and carries out security and medical checks.
  • Local councils or community groups arrange housing and support on arrival.
  • Other schemes exist for specific nationalities; check GOV.UK for current rules.
Programmes, quotas and eligibility rules change often. Always confirm current details with the official government source linked on this page.

Official information: GOV.UK
