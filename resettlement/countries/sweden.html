Resettlement country

Sweden

Sweden resettles refugees under an annual quota decided by the government, with selection by the Swedish Migration Agency.

At a glance

Main programmeQuota refugee resettlement
ReferralsUNHCR submits cases; Sweden also runs selection missions
QuotaSet each year by the Government; it has changed over time
Decision makerSwedish Migration Agency (Migrationsverket)

How it works

  • The government sets the annual quota and priority groups.
  • UNHCR submits cases that match Sweden’s criteria.
  • Migration Agency staff review files and may interview refugees.
  • Those accepted receive residence permits and are placed in a municipality.
Programmes, quotas and eligibility rules change often. Always confirm current details with the official government source linked on this page.

Official information: Swedish Migration Agency
