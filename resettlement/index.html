UNHCR Resettlement

Resettlement creates new beginnings

From crisis to safety. From displacement to opportunity.

How the process worksResettlement countries
Guidance. Information. Support.

UNHCR helps refugees find durable solutions

Durable solutions include resettlement to safer countries, alongside voluntary return and local integration.

Resettlement is for refugees whose lives, safety or health are still at risk where they are. UNHCR identifies who is most in need and refers them to countries that have agreed to admit them.

The resettlement process

A safer future, step by step

Six stages take a refugee from registration to a new home. Timelines vary from months to years.

  1. Registration

    With UNHCR or a partner organization.

  2. Assessment & Referral

    For resettlement to a host country.

  3. Interviews & Screening

    Including document verification, eligibility and security checks.

  4. Approval

    By the resettlement country.

  5. Pre-departure Arrangements

    Travel preparations and orientation.

  6. Arrival & Support

    Housing, healthcare, education, employment and community integration.
