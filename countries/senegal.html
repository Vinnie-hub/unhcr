West & Central Africa

UNHCR in 🇸🇳

UNHCR works in 🇸🇳 together with the government, UN agencies, local and international partners and communities to protect and assist people forced to flee.

Senegal

What UNHCR does

  • Protection. Registering people, documenting them and helping them access asylum and legal protection.
  • Assistance. Shelter, water, health care, education and cash or relief items for families in need.
  • Inclusion. Helping refugees, displaced people and host communities access services and livelihoods together.
  • Solutions. Supporting voluntary return when it is safe, local integration, and resettlement for those most at risk.

Find help

Information and services for refugees, asylum-seekers, stateless and displaced people are on our Get help page. For official, up-to-date country information, visit help.unhcr.org.
