Story

Uganda continues to host Africa's largest refugee population, welcoming those forced to flee

Read more View all news
© UNHCR/Ssozi Daniel Mukasa

UNHCR helps to save lives and build better futures for the millions of people forced to flee their homes.

UNHCR, the UN Refugee Agency works to ensure that everybody has the right to seek asylum and find safe refuge, having fled violence, persecution or war at home.

Uganda hosts over 2 million refugees and asylum-seekers — the largest refugee population in Africa. The majority are from South Sudan (52%) and the Democratic Republic of the Congo (33%), with women and children making up 80% of the population [citation:4][citation:14].

Uganda maintains an open-door policy to asylum and has made significant commitments to refugee inclusion in national systems, including education, health, and livelihoods.

What we do

Respond to emergencies

When conflict and violence force people to flee, UNHCR works with the Government of Uganda and partners to provide timely protection and life-saving assistance. This includes registration, emergency relief, cash assistance, and specialised protection services.

Protection Interventions

UNHCR works to safeguard the rights, safety and dignity of refugees, asylum-seekers, and other forcibly displaced people. Through community-based protection, child protection, and GBV prevention, we help ensure access to essential services and legal support [citation:5][citation:10].

Solutions & Self-Reliance

UNHCR supports refugees to rebuild their lives and become more self-reliant. This includes livelihoods support, skills development, financial inclusion, and creating pathways for voluntary return or local integration in Uganda.
