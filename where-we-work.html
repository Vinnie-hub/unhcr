Where we work

UNHCR works in 136 countries to protect and assist people forced to flee. From emergency responses to long-term solutions, our teams are on the ground wherever they are needed most.

Are you a refugee, asylum-seeker or stateless person looking for help? Visit the HELP site to find support and services from UNHCR.

Reaching people forced to flee, wherever they are.

UNHCR operates in 136 countries across the world, from major emergencies to protracted displacement situations. Our presence on the ground allows us to respond rapidly when crises strike and to stay for as long as we are needed.

We work closely with governments, partner organizations and local communities to deliver protection and assistance. Our staff include experts in protection, shelter, water and sanitation, health, education, livelihoods and more.

In every operation, our goal is the same: to save lives, protect rights and help build better futures for people forced to flee.

Our regional presence

Africa

Africa hosts more than a quarter of the world's refugees. UNHCR works across the continent to protect and assist people forced to flee conflict, persecution and climate-related disasters.

  • Major operations in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan, South Sudan, Ethiopia, Kenya and Chad
  • Support for refugees from Somalia, Burundi, the Central African Republic and beyond
  • Programmes for internally displaced people and host communities
Learn more about Africa
UNHCR supports communities across Africa.

Americas

The Americas face multiple displacement crises, from the Venezuelan exodus to internal displacement in Central America and Colombia.

  • Support for over 7 million Venezuelans displaced across the region
  • Assistance for Central American families fleeing violence and persecution
  • Integration and livelihood programmes for refugees and host communities
Learn more about the Americas
UNHCR works with displaced communities in the Americas.

Asia and the Pacific

Asia hosts some of the world's largest and most protracted refugee situations, including the Rohingya and Afghan displacement crises.

  • Support for nearly 1 million Rohingya refugees in Bangladesh
  • Assistance for Afghans displaced both inside and outside the country
  • Work on statelessness across the region, including in Myanmar and Malaysia
Learn more about Asia and the Pacific
UNHCR responds to displacement across Asia and the Pacific.

Europe

Europe continues to receive people fleeing conflict and persecution, particularly from Ukraine, Syria, Afghanistan and other countries.

  • Humanitarian response for people displaced by the war in Ukraine
  • Support for asylum systems and integration programmes across the EU
  • Assistance for refugees and migrants arriving via Mediterranean routes
Learn more about Europe
UNHCR supports displaced people across Europe.

Middle East and North Africa

The Middle East and North Africa region has been shaped by decades of displacement, including the Syria and Iraq situations and ongoing crises in Yemen and Libya.

  • Support for millions of Syrian refugees in Türkiye, Lebanon, Jordan, Iraq and Egypt
  • Assistance for Yemenis affected by prolonged conflict
  • Protection and assistance for refugees and migrants in North Africa
